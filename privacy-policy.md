# Privacy Policy

**Sa Kyi Health & Wellness**

**Effective Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 15, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to Sa Kyi Health & Wellness ("Sa Kyi", "we", "our", or "us").

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your information when you use our services, including:

- Sa Kyi mobile applications for iOS and Android.
- The Sa Kyi public website.
- Our wellness program enrollment services.
- Our personalized care plans and progress-tracking services.
- Our consultations and communication services.

Sa Kyi Health & Wellness is an online health and wellness service operated by the Sa Kyi team, which also provides wellness-related services through its associated physical operations.

By creating an account, submitting information, requesting program enrollment, or participating in a Sa Kyi program, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

Where applicable law requires specific consent for collecting or processing personal information, we will request that consent separately.

## 2. About Our Services

Sa Kyi provides personalized health and wellness programs designed to help individuals develop healthier daily habits and improve their general well-being.

Our programs may include:

- Nutrition and meal planning.
- Physical exercise and fitness.
- Daily activities and movement.
- Recovery and relaxation.
- Hydration and water-intake goals.
- Sleep and rest routines.
- Meditation and mindfulness.
- Weight management and lifestyle improvement.

Depending on the program and individual circumstances, care plans may be prepared or reviewed by qualified doctors and other wellness professionals.

Our platform operates through three main areas:

**Mobile Application:** Users can create accounts, explore programs, request enrollment, access personalized care plans, record daily activities, and review their progress.

**Public Website:** Visitors can learn about Sa Kyi, explore available programs, and contact our team.

**Administration Dashboard:** Authorized members of our care team manage enrollment requests, onboarding assessments, contracts, care plans, user submissions, and progress reports.

## 3. Information We Collect

We collect information that is reasonably necessary to operate our platform and provide our services.

### 3.1 Personal and Account Information

When you register or complete your profile, we may collect:

- Full name.
- Email address.
- Contact telephone number.
- Date of birth.
- Gender information.
- Contact or residential address.
- Profile photograph, if uploaded.
- Account credentials and authentication information.

We use this information to create your account, communicate with you, and manage your participation in our services.

### 3.2 Onboarding and Personal Goals

During onboarding, we may collect information about your interests, preferences, and wellness goals, including:

- Areas of wellness interest.
- Personal development goals.
- Weight management goals.
- Nutrition preferences.
- Exercise and physical activity interests.
- Sleep and recovery goals.
- Meditation and mindfulness interests.

We use this information to recommend relevant programs and personalize your experience.

### 3.3 Health and Wellness Assessment Information

When you request enrollment, our care team may contact you to arrange an initial consultation and complete an onboarding assessment.

Depending on your program, this may involve collecting:

- Relevant health history or concerns disclosed by you.
- Weight and body measurements.
- Nutrition and eating habits.
- Physical activity and mobility information.
- Food allergies and dietary restrictions.
- Sleep patterns.
- Existing lifestyle habits.
- Personal wellness goals.
- Other relevant information necessary to prepare an appropriate care plan.

This information may be collected directly from you during a consultation and entered into our administration dashboard by an authorized care-team member.

We treat health-related information as sensitive personal information and restrict access to authorized personnel.

### 3.4 Enrollment and Contract Information

When you request to join a program, we may collect:

- Program selection.

- Enrollment status.
- Consultation and assessment information.
- Program start and end dates.

### 3.5 Care Plans and Daily Tracking Information

**Nutrition**

- Breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snack records.

- Nutrition-related notes.

**Exercise**

- Workout information.

The information collected depends on your assigned tasks and the information you submit.

### 3.6 Weekly Reports and Progress Information

Our care team reviews your submitted information and may prepare weekly progress reports.

## 11. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, manage accounts, meet legal obligations, maintain security, and resolve legitimate disputes.

Different information may have different retention periods.

- **Account information:** Retained while your account is active and until deletion is completed.
- **Health assessments:** Retained as necessary for program delivery and applicable legal obligations.
- **Care plans and tracking records:** Retained as necessary to provide and administer your program.
- **Uploaded photographs:** Retained as necessary for the relevant program and then deleted under our media-retention procedures.
- **Enrollment and contract records:** Retained as necessary for contractual administration and legal requirements.
- **Security logs:** Retained as necessary for security and operational purposes.

When information is no longer necessary, it will be deleted or anonymized in accordance with our procedures.

## 12. Account Deletion and Personal Data Removal

You may permanently delete your account through the Delete Account option in the Sa Kyi mobile application.

When you confirm a deletion request:

1. Your account access will be disabled.
2. Authentication sessions and access tokens will be revoked.
3. Your personal account and profile information will be deleted.
4. Your onboarding and wellness assessment information will be deleted.
5. Your enrollment requests and associated program information will be deleted.
6. Your care plans, tracking records, and progress reports will be deleted.
7. Your uploaded photographs, journals, and related content will be deleted.
8. Associated account access will be removed from our administration dashboard.

Certain information may be retained only where required by law or necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Where retention is required, the retained information will be restricted to the relevant purpose and will not be used to restore your deleted account.

Information temporarily present in protected backups may remain until the applicable backup retention cycle expires. It will not be available for ordinary use and will be subject to the deletion process if a backup is restored.

Permanently deleted accounts cannot be restored by Sa Kyi administrators.

We will process deletion requests within our operational timeframe and notify you if additional processing time is required.

Account deletion ends your access to your account, active programs, and associated digital information.
