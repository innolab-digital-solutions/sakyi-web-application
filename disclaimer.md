## 1. General Information

Sa Kyi Health & Wellness provides online health and wellness programs designed to support healthier lifestyle habits, physical activity, nutrition, recovery, sleep, hydration, and general well-being.

The information, recommendations, care plans, educational materials, and other content provided through our website, mobile application, consultations, and programs are intended to support your general health and wellness goals.

Although our care team may include healthcare professionals, our wellness programs are not a substitute for a complete medical examination, diagnosis, medical treatment, or ongoing care from your personal healthcare provider.

## 2. No Guarantee of Medical Outcomes

Every individual has different health circumstances, needs, and responses to lifestyle changes.

Results from participating in a Sa Kyi Health & Wellness program may vary depending on factors such as your health condition, lifestyle, consistency, personal circumstances, and other individual factors.

We do not guarantee specific results, improvements, weight changes, fitness outcomes, or other health-related benefits.

## 3. Personalized Care Plans and Professional Guidance

Depending on your selected program, your onboarding information, consultation, and progress records may be reviewed by members of our care team to help prepare and adjust personalized wellness recommendations.

These recommendations may cover:

- **Nutrition:** General dietary habits, meal planning, and nutrition-related guidance.
- **Exercise:** Physical activity, strength, cardio, flexibility, and fitness activities.
- **Daily Activity:** Walking, movement, meditation, and other lifestyle activities.
- **Recovery:** Rest, relaxation, stretching, and recovery habits.
- **Sleep:** Sleep routines and healthy sleep habits.
- **Hydration:** Daily water intake and hydration habits.
- **Progress Tracking:** Journaling, photographs, activity records, and other progress information.

Where a doctor or other healthcare professional participates, their guidance is provided within their professional role and the scope of the relevant consultation or program.

Personalized wellness recommendations should not be interpreted as a comprehensive medical diagnosis or a replacement for medical treatment. If you have a medical condition or require medical care, consult an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.

## 4. Individual Health Conditions and Medical Advice

Before starting a wellness program or making significant changes to your diet, exercise routine, sleep habits, or other lifestyle activities, consider your existing health circumstances.

You should consult your healthcare provider before participating if you:

- Have an existing medical condition or a history of serious illness.
- Are pregnant, breastfeeding, or recovering from childbirth.
- Have an injury, physical limitation, or mobility concern.
- Take prescription medication or receive ongoing medical treatment.
- Have been advised by a healthcare professional to restrict certain foods or physical activities.
- Are uncertain whether a recommended activity is suitable for you.

Do not stop, change, or replace prescribed medication or medical treatment based on information or recommendations received through our programs.

## 5. Exercise and Physical Activity Risks

Physical activity and exercise involve inherent risks, including muscle soreness, strains, injuries, falls, and other physical complications.

You are responsible for participating in activities according to your physical ability and current health circumstances.

Start activities gradually, follow the instructions provided, and do not attempt exercises that cause significant discomfort or exceed your abilities.

Stop an activity immediately if you experience chest pain, dizziness, fainting, unusual shortness of breath, severe pain, or other concerning symptoms. Seek appropriate medical assistance when necessary.

## 6. Nutrition, Food, and Supplement Information

Nutrition-related information and recommendations provided through our programs are intended to support general wellness and healthier eating habits.

Food requirements and nutritional needs differ between individuals. Information provided through our programs should not be treated as a replacement for individualized medical nutrition therapy where such treatment is required.

Any information or recommendations concerning vitamins, minerals, or other dietary supplements are not a substitute for professional medical advice.

Supplements may have side effects or interact with medications and existing health conditions. Consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting, stopping, or changing supplement use.

## 7. User Responsibility and Accurate Information

You are responsible for providing accurate and complete information during registration, onboarding, consultations, and program participation.

This includes relevant information about your health circumstances, existing conditions, medications, physical limitations, and other factors that may affect your participation.

You should promptly inform the Sa Kyi care team if your health circumstances change or if you experience difficulties or adverse effects while following a care plan.

Incomplete or inaccurate information may affect the suitability of recommendations and the care team's ability to support your wellness goals.

## 8. Personal Progress and Tracking Information

Our platform may allow you to record and share information such as meals, physical activities, sleep hours, hydration, photographs, journal entries, and other progress records.

These records help you and the care team monitor participation and review progress during your program.

Tracking information, calculations, summaries, and progress reports are provided to support wellness monitoring. They should not be treated as medical test results, clinical diagnoses, or proof that a medical condition has improved or worsened.

## 9. Emergencies and Urgent Medical Conditions

Our website, mobile application, messaging features, consultations, and care-team communications are not intended for emergency assistance or continuous medical monitoring.

If you experience a medical emergency, severe symptoms, or a potentially life-threatening condition, contact your local emergency services or go to the nearest appropriate medical facility immediately. Do not wait for a response from Sa Kyi Health & Wellness.
