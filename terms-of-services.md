## 10. Seven-Day Care Plan and Progress Reviews

Sa Kyi generally delivers personalized care plans through structured seven-day tracking cycles.

The process may include:

1. Publication of your weekly care plan.
2. Completion of assigned daily tasks.
3. Submission of tracking values and relevant evidence.
4. Review of submitted information by the care team.
5. Preparation of a weekly progress report.
6. A progress consultation where scheduled.
7. Preparation and publication of the next care plan.

You may be asked to submit:

- Meal photographs.
- Exercise completion records.
- Daily step counts.
- Water-intake values.
- Sleep duration.
- Recovery activity information.
- Personal journal entries.
- Other assigned tracking information.

Your care team reviews available information and may use it to prepare progress summaries and updated recommendations.

The exact schedule may vary depending on your program, consultation availability, individual circumstances, and operational requirements.

A seven-day cycle is the general structure of our program delivery and does not guarantee that every report or consultation will occur at an identical time.
